# CPD Compass — Privacy statement

Last updated: October 2026

**CPD Compass collects no personal information.**
- Everything you enter — your profile, CPD records, plans and certificate photos — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no third-party advertising, no tracking cookies, and no server that receives your data. The publisher cannot see your records.
- The app includes an optional “Say thanks” section linking to novels by the developer's wife, author Samantha Grosser. Those links are not tracked, earn no commission, and only open if you tap them. Book covers are stored inside the app, so viewing them contacts no one.
- The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may log basic technical data (e.g. IP address) when the app is downloaded — see GitHub's privacy statement.
- **AI features are optional.** If you add your own AI key, the text (and any certificate image) you choose to send goes directly from your device to that provider (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) under their terms. Your key is stored only on your device and is never included in backups. Never send patient-identifying information.
- Backups are files you create and control. Store them somewhere secure.
- Links in the Free learning directory take you to other organisations' websites, which have their own privacy policies.
- To remove all data, use **Delete all data on this device** in Settings, or delete the app.
